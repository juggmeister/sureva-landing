Questions, answered.

Does it replace sunscreen?

No. You still choose and apply your own sunscreen. Sureva tells you when it’s time to put more on.

How does it know my sunscreen has worn off?

It works it out. Every 30 seconds it reads UV, temperature, humidity, movement and water, and combines them with the SPF and water resistance you entered. Each condition wears sunscreen down at its own rate, and each swim takes a bite out of it. When the estimate drops low enough for your skin type, it tells you.

What if it’s a mild day?

It never lets more than two hours go by without a reminder, however gentle the conditions. On most active days it will ask sooner.

What happens if I miss the alert?

It asks again 15 minutes later, and once more at 30, until you confirm you’ve reapplied.

Where do I wear it?

Anywhere the sun reaches you: a hat brim, a shoulder strap, your chest or upper arm. It can sit on your wrist too, and it allows for the extra sun your wrist catches.

Can I swim with it?

Yes. It’s sealed, waterproof and has no ports. It also notices when you go in, because water is one of the fastest ways to lose protection.

Is Sureva a medical device?

No. It’s a general wellness product that helps you remember to reapply sunscreen and keep track of your UV exposure. It doesn’t diagnose, treat or prevent any condition.

What data does it collect?

UV readings, temperature, humidity, movement and water events from the device, the SPF and skin type you enter, and your location if you allow it. This data isn’t protected by HIPAA. The privacy policy, published before launch, will set out exactly what’s kept, who it’s shared with and how to delete it.
